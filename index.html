Career I Ching Reader
易經 · Career reading

Ask the I Ching about your career

Type your question in open style and keep peaceful mind. The app opens iching.scylla.cc, picks the career category, and brings back the hexagram reading with three questions for reflection, in Chinese and English. An I Ching AI master then explains what it means for you in plain words, draws it as a mind map, and turns it into an illustrated poster.

AI settings Used outside Claude: Gemini writes the translation and analysis, and draws the poster

Your key is kept only in this browser and sent only to Google. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.

  1. 01Take your question
  2. 02Open the I Ching site and press 「我準備好了，開始問卦」
  3. 03Choose 事業・工作 (career), enter the question, press 「靜心起卦」
  4. 04Read the hexagram, guidance and three reflection questions
  5. 05Show the result in Traditional Chinese and English
  6. 06I Ching master explains the reading 易經師父解卦
  7. 07Draw a mind map of the master's reading 心智圖
  8. 08Draw an English poster of the mind map 心智圖海報

Your reading will appear here: the hexagram number and name, the site's guidance on the situation, its dynamic, your blind spot and your position, a career-specific note, and three open questions for reflection. Press Cast to try the example question.

Readings come from iching.scylla.cc in Traditional Chinese. Claude writes the English translation and the master's analysis, or Gemini when the page is opened outside Claude. The Step 8 poster is in English, drawn by Claude inside Claude and by Gemini outside Claude. The site casts at random, so each cast can give a different hexagram. Use the reading for reflection, not as a prediction.
